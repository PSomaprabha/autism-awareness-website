Support & Guidance for You

🧠 Recommended Therapies

  • Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA): Improves communication, learning & behavior.
  • Speech Therapy: Helps with language, articulation, and communication.
  • Occupational Therapy: Builds daily life skills like dressing, eating, etc.
  • Social Skills Training: Enhances interaction and friendships.

💡 Daily Tips & Suggestions

  • Create a daily routine with visual cues.
  • Use calm tones and clear communication.
  • Encourage interests and hobbies.
  • Celebrate small wins and improvements!
