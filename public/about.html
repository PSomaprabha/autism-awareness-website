About Autism Awareness

What is Autism?

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a developmental condition that affects how a person communicates, interacts, and behaves. It is called a "spectrum" because it affects individuals differently, ranging from mild to severe. Early diagnosis and therapy can significantly improve the quality of life for individuals with autism.

Levels of Autism

Mild Autism

Individuals may have slight difficulties with social interactions and may exhibit repetitive behaviors, but they can lead independent lives with minimal support.

Moderate Autism

People may struggle more with communication and daily activities, requiring structured support and therapies to improve social and adaptive skills.

Severe Autism

Individuals may face significant challenges with speech, understanding, and self-care, needing lifelong support and intensive interventions.
